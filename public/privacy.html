Privacy Policy

hantamaps.com is a live hantavirus and Andes virus outbreak tracker operated at https://hantamaps.com/. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

What We Collect

This website does not collect personally identifiable information. Your language preference and cached outbreak data are stored locally in your browser's localStorage only — nothing is sent to our servers. Basic access logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) may be retained by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, for security and performance purposes.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and localStorage for two purposes only:

  • Remembering your language preference across visits.
  • Caching outbreak data locally so the map loads faster on return visits.

A cookie consent banner is shown on first visit. You may accept or reject non-essential storage at any time via the banner.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used and may process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN, and DDoS protection. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • ipapi.co — approximate country detection from your IP address to automatically select a display language. No data is stored by us. Only used if you have not already chosen a language. ipapi.co Privacy Policy.
  • GNews — live hantavirus news headlines displayed in the right-side news panel. Article data is fetched client-side. GNews Privacy Policy.
  • MapLibre GL JS / OpenStreetMap tiles — open-source map rendering. Tile requests may be logged by the tile provider.

We do not use advertising networks, social media trackers, or analytics platforms such as Google Analytics.

Data Sources

Outbreak data is sourced from publicly available reports published by the World Health Organization (WHO), the CDC, the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC), PAHO, and national health ministries. No private or patient-level data is ever collected or displayed.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS via Cloudflare. No user accounts, login forms, or payment flows exist on this website. There is no backend database storing personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of 2026-05-11. We may update it to reflect changes in services or legal requirements. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy can be sent to hantamapmail@gmail.com.
