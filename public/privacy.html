Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-05-12  ·  hantamaps.com

Overview

hantamaps.com is a live hantavirus and Andes virus outbreak tracker. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

What We Collect

This website does not collect personally identifiable information. Your language preference and cached outbreak data are stored locally in your browser's localStorage only — nothing is sent to our servers. Basic access logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) may be retained by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, for security and performance purposes.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and localStorage for the following purposes:

  • Remembering your language preference across visits.
  • Caching outbreak data locally so the map loads faster on return visits.
  • Google Analytics measurement cookies (only if you have consented via the cookie banner).
  • Google AdSense advertising cookies (only if you have consented via the cookie banner).

A cookie consent banner is shown on first visit via Cookiebot. You may accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time. Rejecting advertising cookies means ads may still appear but will not be personalised.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used and may process data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN, and DDoS protection. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymous usage analytics including page views, session duration, and approximate location. Consent is required before activation. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cookiebot — cookie consent management. Cookiebot Privacy Policy.
  • ipapi.co — approximate country detection from your IP address to automatically select a display language. No data is stored by us. Only used if you have not already chosen a language. ipapi.co Privacy Policy.
  • GNews — live hantavirus news headlines displayed in the news panel. Article data is fetched client-side. GNews Privacy Policy.
  • MapLibre GL JS / OpenStreetMap tiles — open-source map rendering. Tile requests may be logged by the tile provider.
  • Google AdSense — this site may display ads served by Google. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings.

Data Sources

Outbreak data is sourced from publicly available reports by the World Health Organization (WHO), CDC, ECDC, PAHO, and national health ministries. No private or patient-level data is ever collected or displayed.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS via Cloudflare. No user accounts, login forms, or payment flows exist on this website. There is no backend database storing personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This policy is effective as of 2026-05-12. We may update it to reflect changes in services or legal requirements. Material changes will be noted on this page with an updated effective date.
