About hantamaps.com

hantamaps.com is an independent, non-commercial outbreak tracker monitoring the 2026 Andes hantavirus outbreak linked to the MV Hondius cruise ship. The site displays country-level confirmed cases, suspected cases, deaths, and monitoring status on a live interactive globe, updated every 6 hours.

What We Track

This site tracks the current MV Hondius / Andes hantavirus outbreak specifically — not historical hantavirus presence worldwide. Countries are classified as confirmed, suspected, or under monitoring based on reports from official public health authorities.

Data Sources

All outbreak data is sourced exclusively from publicly available official reports:

  • World Health Organization (WHO) Disease Outbreak News
  • U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
  • European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC)
  • Pan American Health Organization (PAHO)
  • National health ministries and official government sources

Data is aggregated automatically every 6 hours. No private, patient-level, or personally identifiable information is collected or displayed.

Who Operates This Site

hantamaps.com is operated independently as a public information resource. It is not affiliated with any government, health authority, pharmaceutical company, or media organization.

Disclaimer

This site is for informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Case counts and classifications may lag official figures as sources are updated. Always consult your local public health authority or a qualified medical professional for health guidance.
